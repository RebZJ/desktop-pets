# Give Pace a life outside the repo

The proposition: **a little desktop friend that helps you get things done**. Lead with a useful action and Pace's reaction in the same shot. Local processing is a concrete product property to demonstrate.

## First three experiments

1. **My computer adopted a coworker.** A 20-second clip: Pace waves hello, rewrites an awkward reply, types during a synthetic meeting, celebrates and sprints away when hidden. End with the open-source repository and a download link.
2. **Bring your own little helper.** Publish the pet-pack starter manifest, show a community pet taking the same actions and invite a remix pull request. Maintain a small credited gallery in the repository. Test whether the remix spreads beyond developer audiences.
3. **An offline meeting companion.** Disconnect networking after the model download, play the labelled synthetic meeting and show the transcript, speaker labels and source-timed notes. A second clip demonstrates English, Mandarin, Japanese and Spanish voices.

## Reusable assets

- Twelve original sprite actions and a playable in-app gallery.
- A short synthetic meeting fixture generator and transparent validation notes.
- The pet-pack format, CC BY attribution and contribution instructions.
- Honest release notes listing supported hardware and permission requirements.

Track public downloads, useful bug reports, remix submissions and voluntary seven-day feedback. Do not add background telemetry just to run these experiments. A mascot can earn attention; repeat use depends on the writing and meeting workflows helping people.

No posts or outreach are sent by this build. Prepare demos using synthetic content; publish launch communications deliberately when the release is ready.

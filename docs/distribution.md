# Give Pace a life outside the repo

The proposition: **a little desktop friend that helps you get things done**. Lead with a useful action and Pace's reaction in the same shot. Local processing is a concrete product property to demonstrate.

## First three experiments

1. **My computer adopted a coworker.** A 20-second clip: Pace rests alone, reveals his weapon selector on hover, grows a comic ear while dictating into a synthetic field, then suggests one short continuation for explicit acceptance. End with the open-source repository and a download link.
2. **Bring your own little helper.** Publish the pet-pack starter manifest, show a community pet taking the same actions and invite a remix pull request. Maintain a small credited gallery in the repository. Test whether the remix spreads beyond developer audiences.
3. **An offline meeting companion.** Disconnect networking after the model download, play the labelled synthetic meeting and show the transcript, speaker labels and source-timed notes. A second clip demonstrates English, Mandarin, Japanese and Spanish voices.

## Reusable assets

- Fifteen sprite actions, including new talk/listen/big-ear poses, plus an in-app gallery and remixable starter ZIP.
- A short synthetic meeting fixture generator and transparent validation notes.
- The pet-pack format, CC BY attribution and contribution instructions.
- Honest release notes listing supported hardware and permission requirements.

Track public downloads, useful bug reports, remix submissions and voluntary seven-day feedback. Do not add background telemetry just to run these experiments. A mascot can earn attention; repeat use depends on fast dictation, helpful cursor continuations and meeting workflows actually helping people.

No posts or outreach are sent by this build. Prepare demos using synthetic content; publish launch communications deliberately when the release is ready.

The strongest first demo is the quiet-to-useful transition: a bare pet, a satisfying hover selector, a big listening ear, then words in the user’s field. Show the real interaction and access setup, using synthetic content. The small dictation download is a useful installation hook; chat and voice are optional upgrades. Test these angles rather than claiming they will increase retention or downloads.

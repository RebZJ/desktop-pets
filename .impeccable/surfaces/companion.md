# Pace lives on the desktop

Mode: Experience for the pet and tool cloud; Operate for settings and meeting archives. Shipping surface: Tauri native transparent windows, with browser rendering used for contract/layout tests.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Work feels tangible through a little companion: click Talk beneath Pace, dictate into the app you are using, or ask for a short continuation at your cursor; see him listen, think and speak.

**OWN-WORLD:** The user's explicit GBA/8-bit direction now spans the whole app. Forest outlines, cream dialogue, brick-red selections, square controls, Silkscreen lettering, pixel sprites and stepped speech tails form one world. Editable prose and transcripts remain readable.

**STORY:** Install the needed models first; choose and practice shortcuts; meet Pace on the desktop. Talk, dictate, or choose Write for me; review a continuation before accepting it. The main workspace supports access, settings and reviewing longer work. Existing saved conversations and meetings stay available.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Pace floats in a transparent native window alone at rest. Hovering the sprite fades in a three-slot weapon selector beneath him: Talk, Dictate and Write for me. Keyboard focus reveals the same controls. Each hovered slot lifts and grows slightly, its neighbours respond, and reduced motion removes movement. A Talk cloud (420×330) anchors beside the pet; an autocomplete preview (420×240) appears above the captured cursor, clamped to the monitor. Accept inserts only into the unchanged original field; Copy and Dismiss are explicit fallbacks. There is no continuous background autocomplete or primary rewrite workbench.

**FORM:** Code-led expansion of the user's specified pixel-game world. No concept tournament, seed or approved comp. The user's screenshots establish the conversation-layout defect and the intent to move writing into a cloud beside Pace.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Interaction and truth

Talk, recording, model downloads, voice and autocomplete Accept require explicit actions. Global dictation checks desktop access before recording and types into the unchanged captured field; successful recording uses the pet’s ear state without opening another cloud; Talk's microphone fills its draft for review before Send. Failed insertion preserves a copyable transcript. Streamed text belongs to its client request. Playback drives talking; a microphone session drives the big-ear dictation pose. New talking/listening/dictation strips match the original art's floor and scale. Reduced motion freezes sprite animation.

Clouds preserve focus and scrollable content; passive meeting tips do not interrupt an active writing cloud. Quick hide hides pet and companion windows. Shortcut activation can summon Pace again. A browser preview cannot perform native inference or access and says so.

MLX converted weights and voice embeddings:

English pronunciation resources:
beshkenadze/kitten-tts-g2p at 9c692b92682d959d9013a9cfe6a49541997add18.
The resource card declares MIT metadata and says it was ported from Misaki.
The card's description of upstream Misaki as MIT conflicts with Misaki's
actual Apache-2.0 license, which already existed before this port.
Upstream Misaki: https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki/tree/fba1236595f2d2bf21d414ba6e57d25256afada3
Full upstream license: Misaki-Apache-2.0.txt.
The installer retains the pinned resource card alongside all US English
lexicons, BART weights and configuration. These resources are not described
as MIT-only in the app's distribution notices.

Swift runtime: mlx-audio-swift at df2a2546705f50ca267037702a34cd63aa6e35a6.
Its full license is retained separately in swift-packages/mlx-audio-swift.
Local change: native/patches/mlx-audio-kitten-istft.patch materializes the
inverse-STFT frames once, preserving the existing overlap-add calculation.
The second local patch, native/patches/mlx-audio-misaki-offline.patch, adds
an explicit local pronunciation directory that never falls back to network
preparation. This app validates the complete bundle before creating it.
The local context guard in native/patches/mlx-audio-kitten-context.patch
rejects overlong phoneme sequences before the fixed embedding lookup.
